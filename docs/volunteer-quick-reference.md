# NovaRace Flow: volunteer quick reference

Keep this page at the counter. Your organiser supplies the scanner link and pairing code.

### Before the first guest

1. Open the live NovaRace Flow scanner link in Chrome. If pairing is required, enter Tablet name and the organiser's fresh 8-digit code, then choose Pair this tablet.
2. Check the event and gate names. Connect your QR-capable scanner in HID keyboard mode with an Enter suffix, or choose Camera and allow access.
3. Test a pass with the organiser. Keep the tablet charged, the scanner screen open, and Ready to scan visible.

### For each guest

Ask for the event QR pass. Scan once, check the name, and follow the result below. A scanner beep is not approval.

| Result                     | What you do                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| GREEN - confirmed          | Complete the gate action for this guest.                                                                    |
| AMBER - already collected  | Check the earlier time. Do not issue again; ask the event lead if disputed.                                 |
| RED - not accepted         | Read the reason. Direct the guest to the correct previous gate or organiser help desk.                      |
| AMBER - pending validation | Saved locally, not approved yet. Wait for confirmation or follow the lead's explicit provisional procedure. |

### Offline or connection problem

Keep the same app and tablet. Reconnect, leave NovaRace Flow open, tap Sync now, wait for 0 queued, and review Recent results. Offline scans cannot check other tablets' collections until syncing.

> **Protect queued scans**
>
> Do not clear site data, uninstall, remove pairing or use Incognito. If a scan was NOT saved, stop and call the event lead. Online alone does not mean the queue has synced.

### Need help?

Scanner silent or text not submitted? Focus the scanner input; check HID mode and Enter suffix. Camera blocked? Ask the setup team to check camera permission and HTTPS. Broken phone? Verify identity and get approval before Manual override. It needs an online lookup and a reason, and can bypass limits and gate order.

Before handover or shutdown: confirm 0 queued and report unresolved scans to the event lead. Volunteers do not need the organiser password. Do not share your tablet's access outside the event team.
